# 经营者的财务金三角 - 阅读指南

## 📖 Recommended Reading Path

### Option 1: Linear Approach (按序阅读)
Best for: Building foundational understanding from scratch

**Week 1-2: Module 1 (Operations)**
- Read: Preface + Chapter 1-5
- Focus: Understand profit concepts, cost structures, asset thinking
- Goal: Answer "How does my company make money?"

**Week 3-4: Module 2 (Financing)**
- Read: Chapter 11-16
- Focus: Cash flow, capital sources, debt capacity
- Goal: Answer "Where does my company get money?"

**Week 5: Module 3 (Investment)**
- Read: Chapter 19-26
- Focus: Asset allocation, growth strategies, investment decisions
- Goal: Answer "Where does my company deploy money?"

**Week 6: Integration**
- Review all three modules
- Create your company's golden triangle assessment

---

### Option 2: Context-Driven Approach (按问题阅读)

**If you're focused on PROFITABILITY**:
→ Read Chapters 1-4, 6, 8-9

**If you're focused on CASH FLOW**:
→ Read Chapters 11-17, especially 13 (Black Swan Closure)

**If you're focused on GROWTH**:
→ Read Chapters 5, 19-26

**If you're focused on VALUATION**:
→ Read Chapters 12, 24-26

## 🎯 Key Learning Milestones

### Level 1: Conceptual Understanding (Ch 1-4)
- [ ] Understand dimensional reduction concept
- [ ] Know the core financial metrics
- [ ] Grasp the difference between profit and cash flow
- [ ] Recognize profit as a constructed (man-made) concept

**Time: 1 week**
**Difficulty: ⭐⭐**

### Level 2: Operational Mastery (Ch 5-10)
- [ ] Understand asset classification and impact
- [ ] Know how to analyze cost structures
- [ ] Master relevant vs sunk cost thinking
- [ ] Apply these to your business decisions

**Time: 2 weeks**
**Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐**

### Level 3: Financial Structure (Ch 11-18)
- [ ] Understand capital sources and cash flow
- [ ] Know the dangers of profitable insolvency
- [ ] Master debt capacity analysis
- [ ] Analyze your company's financing strategy

**Time: 2 weeks**
**Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐**

### Level 4: Investment Strategy (Ch 19-26)
- [ ] Understand asset-heavy vs asset-light models
- [ ] Master growth strategy financial analysis
- [ ] Know how to evaluate investments
- [ ] Create strategic financial scenarios

**Time: 2 weeks**
**Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐⭐**

## 💡 Study Tips

### 1. **Connect to Real Business**
- As you read, think about companies you know
- Ask: "Does this explain what happened to [Company X]?"
- Use examples to test understanding

### 2. **Create Your Own Examples**
- Don't just memorize the author's examples
- Create examples from your own industry
- Build mental library of applications

### 3. **Build Integration Map**
- Keep a visual map showing how chapters connect
- See how each dimension (Operations/Financing/Investment) interconnects
- Understand the holistic framework, not isolated concepts

### 4. **Active Questioning**
- Before reading each chapter, generate questions
- After reading, try to answer without looking back
- See what stuck vs. what needs re-reading

### 5. **Teach Someone Else**
- Explain each chapter's main idea to a colleague
- Try to convince them of its importance
- Teaching reveals gaps in understanding
