## 📝 Notes

**Author's Teaching Philosophy**: 
This book is NOT about accounting principles. Instead, it's written for business operators who want to understand finance from an operational perspective. The author helps operators decode financial statements and think in terms of business strategy, not accounting rules.

**Original Context**: 
First book published 2013 as "Manager's 14 Days to Read Financial Reports". This 2021 edition refines and expands the framework as "The Financial Golden Triangle for Operators" - giving equal weight to operations, financing, and investment perspectives.

**Key Insight from Author**:
> 经营者需要维到财务这一维度，采用以终为始的方式进行经营决策，同时需要通过财务仪表盘来掌控公司；财务人员则需要升维到业务层面，只有找到财务指标的引导性指标（经营行为指标），他们才能真正帮助经营者改善公司的业绩。

**Translation**: Operators need to think at the financial dimension and make strategic decisions with financial end-goals in mind, while using financial dashboards to control the company. Finance staff need to elevate to the business level - only by finding leading indicators of financial metrics (operational behavior indicators) can they truly help operators improve company performance.
