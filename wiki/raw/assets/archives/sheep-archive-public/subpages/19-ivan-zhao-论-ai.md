他实现 tools for thoughts 的方式是幸运和持久的，但是产品想象力上逐渐脱离早期的天才，均值回归。

最近反复看我在很长一段时间关注最多的 2 个人。

Mike Matas ，心目中第一的 UI 设计师，小时候因为对文字障碍，转去界面设计，如今和日裔妻子、2个女儿，十只鸡，蜜蜂，和丰盛的蔬菜园在隐居旧金山郊区，偶尔接一下 Jony Ive 爵士的设计外包。

Bret Victor，心目中第一的 交互设计师，先学电气工程，找寻人生的原则和意义，经历 10 年的迷茫，如今独立研究一个能百年后还能使用的看得见的系统和一个新的媒介。

关于宇宙、生命和人工智能

宇宙根本不在乎

但生命（比如人类）会在乎，而且确实有自己的价值观

所有生物（变形虫/花朵/人类）如果有条件，都想活得更好——这就是生命的本能价值观

生命的意义就是对抗冷漠宇宙，坚持自己的价值观

每个生命都受限于自身条件和时代，但生命会联合起来搞发明创造

花朵靠蜜蜂传播花粉，用花蜜作为报酬；细胞和细菌结合，省去电能，最终进化出线粒体

人类用文化工具加速发现这些生存技巧，让宇宙越来越符合生命的价值观

轮子比腿更能搬运重物，这种工具不是单细胞生物能发明的，但人类用大脑+木头/石头就搞定了

金钱资本也是工具，它们像轮子一样传递人类需求和能量信息，甚至比实物储存更高效

语言/梗/歌曲/数学也是工具，像蜜蜂跳舞传递信息那样，帮人类跨时空分享生存技巧

这些人类发明的工具就像"非肉体生命体"，和血肉生命一起改造宇宙

所有生命都在竞争生存资源：甲壳虫汽车、昆虫甲壳虫、披头士乐队都在争夺地球母亲的能量

人口增长带来更多智慧：更多蜜蜂和人类意味着更多大脑和电脑来发明新工具

多样性是生命联盟的战斗力：不同物种分工合作，才能更好对抗冷漠宇宙

工业革命就是典型例子：用煤炭石油这些"远古储存的阳光"，突破了一年一季的太阳能限制

科学让我们不再依赖传说，而是用事实建造更坚固的文明大厦

城市和书籍就像思想酒吧，互联网是24小时营业的——让不同大脑的思想能随时碰撞

物质工具+思维工具共同改造宇宙，让它更适合生命生存

现在轮到人工智能登场了

英文原文

On universe, life, and AI

The universe doesn't care.

Life, such as humans, cares and does have a value system.

If possible, all amoebas, flowers, and humans would want to live like kings. That is the value system of life.

The quest for life is to exert that value against this cold universe that doesn't care.

In this quest, each life and human is confined within their own reach and age. But life has this magic to summon more life, and discover tricks and trades among the alliance of lives — to arrange the energy of the universe according to their value.

Flowers borrow legs from bees to spread, and give pollen as food in exchange. Or, cells and bacterias marry to save on plumbing bills, and become mitochondria.

Humans accelerate the discovery of these tricks and trades with culture and tools, sculpting the universe further towards life and humanity's value.

Tools like wheels transport heavy loads of atoms better than legs. Wheels didn't happen with meatball lives, but it finally happened with human brains and clever arrangements of wood and stone.

Money and capital are also tools and tricks. They are the wheels and legs that convey information about energy and human preferences, and often store energy better than atoms.

Languages, memes, songs, and math are like that too. They shepherd human tricks and trades through space and time. They are humans' bee-dance to tell where the pollen is, has been, or might be going to be.

Together, those human-created tools and tricks are like non-meatball forms of life with varying degrees of vitality. They join the alliance with the rest of meatball life to spread life's value in this cold universe.

Among this alliance of life, competition is the shared love language. The VW Beetle is competing with insect beetles and the Beatles' songs for Mother Life's attention and her milk of energy.

Population growth expands this alliance of life. More bees and more humans bring more brains and GPUs to come up more tools, tricks, and trades. More life means more.

Diversity strengthens this alliance of life. In fact, diversity is the very vitality of this union. It allows for a finer division of labor to organize the citizens of this alliance against the indifferent universe.

The Industrial Revolution is one such trick of organization. Fire, iron, and buried batteries from past lives free the current lives from the food stamps of the yearly sun. This momentary creative freedom breeds more brains and memes to unearth more tricks and treasures.

Science is another trick of organization. It orients brains and memes away from stories and opinions, but towards facts. And facts are the steel that made skyscrapers of memes possible, no more house of cards.

Cities and books are another such organizational trick. They are the pubs where brains and memes meet, so brain-bees can travel less to mix the pollen of memes. The internet and Reddit are the pubs on weekends.

Tools for atoms and tools for thoughts arrange the universe in favor of humanity and life's value system.

And here comes AI.
